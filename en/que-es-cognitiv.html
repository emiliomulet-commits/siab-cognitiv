THE ARCHITECTURE

Five modules. One box.

COGNITIV is not a chatbot: it is a complete sovereign AI system. Each module covers one layer of the promise — intelligence, governance, orchestration, action and proof.

COREInference engine

Runs open models (Llama, Mistral…) locally, compute-to-data. 100% in-house inference, no external API. You choose which model and which version.

VAULTData and permissions

Data governance by design: role-based permissions with ODRL (UNE 0087), the rule travels attached to the data. Encryption and least-privilege access: each person sees only what their role allows.

HUBVector orchestrator

Unifies ERP, CRM, warehouse and documents in a vector memory. One question spans the whole operation. It federates inside the company and beyond, with customers and suppliers, without handing over data.

NEXUSAgents and memory

Agents that carry out complete tasks within the limits you set. And a living corporate memory: knowledge stays in the company, even when people change.

TRACETraceability and audit

Every question, every answer and every action is recorded in full and can be consulted. AI stops being a black box: what cannot be audited cannot be governed.
