13VECTOR ORCHESTRATOR

Vector orchestrator

One question about the whole business: intelligence across the entire operation, not a single document.

The AI Server is a vector orchestrator for all data, internal and external. It unifies information from ERP, CRM, warehouse and production, as well as from customers and suppliers, in a single vector memory. The user asks one question in natural language and gets an answer that draws on all those sources at once. It is not an assistant that answers about one document: it is intelligence across the company's entire operation.

Why it matters. A company's information lives scattered across silos that do not talk to each other. Answering a business question usually means cross-checking several systems by hand. The vector orchestrator removes that work: it turns all the data into a memory that can be queried in one go.

14FEDERATED INSIDE AND OUT

Federated inside and out

The same node orchestrates your company inside and your value chain outside.

The AI Server works in a federated way in two directions. Inwards, it connects every member of the organisation with all its systems, giving a cross-functional view of the business. Outwards, it federates with customers and suppliers: organisations share knowledge without sharing data, and each one remains the owner of its own. The same node operates in both directions.

Why it matters. Companies need to collaborate with their ecosystem —suppliers, customers— but they cannot and do not want to hand over their data. Federation resolves that tension: it enables real collaboration without giving up data sovereignty, something impossible with traditional approaches.

15CORPORATE MEMORY THAT STAYS

Corporate memory that stays

The company's knowledge stays in the company, even when people change.

The AI Server builds a living memory of the organisation: procedures, decisions, history, criteria. That knowledge stops living only in the heads of a few people and becomes a searchable asset that remains when someone retires, changes jobs or leaves. The company stops losing know-how every time it loses a key person.

Why it matters. Knowledge drain is a silent cost: when a veteran leaves, their judgement and context leave with them. A searchable corporate memory turns that tacit know-how into a permanent asset of the company, not of the individual.

16SPECIALISED IN YOUR BUSINESS

Specialised in your business

Not a generic AI: it learns your vocabulary, your products and the way you work.

The AI Server draws on the customer's own data and documents, so its answers are anchored in the reality of their business: their product references, customers, processes and terminology. It does not improvise with general knowledge: it reasons over the company's real information. It is the difference between an assistant that knows a bit about everything and one that knows your company specifically.

Why it matters. A generic AI gives general answers, and in a business context that is not enough —and sometimes it makes things up. Anchoring intelligence in the customer's own data makes the answers useful, precise and reliable, because they speak the company's language.

17AUTONOMOUS AGENTS UNDER CONTROL

Autonomous agents under control

AI does not just answer: it carries out complete tasks, always within the limits you set.

The AI Server can deploy agents that run processes from start to finish: preparing an order, checking an invoice, generating a report, cross-checking an incident. They do not just answer; they act. But each agent operates within explicit limits: what it can consult, what it can execute and what it is forbidden to do. Real automation, but with the guardrail always on and everything logged.

Why it matters. The value of AI lies not only in answering but in taking repetitive work off people's hands. Agents do that, but the key is control: automation without clear limits is frightening, and rightly so. Here the customer defines exactly how far each agent can go.

18MULTIMODAL AND MULTILINGUAL

Multimodal and multilingual

It understands documents, spreadsheets, emails and languages: all your information, not just clean text.

A company's information is not just neat text: it is PDFs, spreadsheets, emails, scanned delivery notes, record sheets, and often in several languages. The AI Server is built to understand that heterogeneous material and work with it in a unified way. A single intelligence that reads what the company actually produces, in the format and language in which it produces it.

Why it matters. Much of a company's useful knowledge is trapped in awkward formats and different languages. An AI that only understands clean text leaves most of it out. Multimodal and multilingual capability unlocks information that until now no one could consult quickly.
