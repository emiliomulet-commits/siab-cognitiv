25EUROPEAN DIGITAL SOVEREIGNTY

European digital sovereignty

Aligned with Europe's strategy: strategic autonomy in AI and data, not external dependence.

Europe has made digital sovereignty a strategic priority: depending less on foreign technology for its data and its intelligence. The AI Server embodies that principle at company scale —data inside, open models, European standards— and aligns the customer with the direction set by European regulation and public funding. Buying sovereignty is not just a technical decision: it means being on the right side of a deep-seated trend.

Why it matters. Digital sovereignty is already a criterion that carries weight in regulation, public procurement and European funding. A solution aligned with that framework not only complies better: it opens doors to grants, tenders and a stronger position with customers and institutions.

26GEOPOLITICAL INDEPENDENCE

Geopolitical independence

Your intelligence is not at the mercy of decisions by another country, another law or another company.

When a company's AI lives in a foreign provider's cloud, it is subject to the laws, sanctions and commercial decisions of that country and that company. A distant regulatory change, geopolitical tension or a unilateral decision by the provider can change access or terms overnight. With your own infrastructure, that exposure disappears: the capability is physically at the customer's site, out of reach of other people's decisions.

Why it matters. Dependence on foreign providers has proved to be a real risk: cross-border data access laws, sanctions, export restrictions, policy changes. For a strategic capability, being exposed to decisions taken elsewhere and without your say is a business vulnerability.

27USE CASES BY SECTOR

Use cases by sector

The same principle, different value in each sector: industry, healthcare, finance, legal and public sector.

Data sovereignty is not an abstract argument: it lands differently in each sector. In industry, it protects manufacturing secrets and unifies the plant. In healthcare, it keeps patient data inside and makes GDPR compliance easier. In finance, it helps with DORA and confidentiality. In legal, it preserves professional secrecy. In the public sector, it guarantees sovereignty and traceability. One product, a sharp value proposition for each audience.

Why it matters. A generic argument is less convincing than one that speaks to the customer's specific pain. Knowing how sovereignty lands in each sector lets sales move from talking about the product to talking about the customer's problem, which is where deals are closed.

28SCALES WITH YOU

Scales with you

You start with one case and grow module by module: a roadmap, not a leap in the dark.

There is no need to transform the company all at once. The AI Server is deployed in phases: you start with a use case with clear value, prove the return and keep expanding —more sources, more areas, more agents, external federation— in a modular, controlled way. A roadmap that grows with the customer, not an all-or-nothing project.

Why it matters. Big technology projects are daunting —and fail— when they are framed as a leap in the dark. A modular rollout reduces risk, shows value early and lets management decide each expansion on real results, not promises.

29EFFICIENT AND SUSTAINABLE

Efficient and sustainable

Compute sized to your real needs: less waste, more control, a footprint you manage.

The AI Server is sized for the customer's real needs and runs models efficiently on-premise, without the overhead of sending every query to a remote data centre. The customer controls and can optimise their own consumption, instead of relying on a third party's opaque efficiency. Energy efficiency and footprint control become one more variable the customer governs.

Why it matters. Sustainability is already a purchasing and reporting criterion for many companies. A local, right-sized system under your control makes it possible to measure and optimise consumption, unlike the opacity of the cloud. It is an argument that resonates with management, procurement and sustainability teams.

30A PARTNER, NOT A VENDOR

A partner, not a vendor

We do not sell a box and disappear: we deploy, support you and stand by you.

The AI Server is not a product that is delivered and forgotten. Behind it there is ongoing support: use-case analysis, integration with the customer's systems, training for their team, support and evolution. The customer owns the intelligence, and we stay on to make it work and keep delivering value. Ownership for the customer, shared responsibility for making it work: that is the relationship.

Why it matters. The difference between AI that works and AI that ends up in a drawer is almost never the model: it is the support. A good partner reduces deployment risk, speeds up value and gives management peace of mind. It is often the factor that tips the decision.
