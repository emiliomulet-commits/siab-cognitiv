How we choose your server

Three questions decide the size. We answer them with you during the assessment.

Which AI model will you use?

A compact model fits on one or two GPUs. Large generative models need eight-GPU servers with high-speed links between them.

How many people at once?

One department or the whole company. The number of simultaneous queries determines how many GPUs and how much memory are needed.

Where will it be installed?

Data centre, technical room, branch office or plant floor. Outside the data centre, a compact, quiet edge unit is the best fit.
